# The Meteorite Kids

A pixel turn-based RPG about five kids who find meteorite stones that give them superpowers. Play solo or team up online with friends.

Play it in your browser: https://ncstateguy.github.io/meteorite-kids/

Saves are kept in your own browser. To move them to another device, use **Move saves** on the main menu.

## Playing online

1. One person clicks **Online**, then **Host a game**, and shares the 5-letter room code.
2. Friends open the same link, click **Online**, enter the code and click **Join**.
3. Each friend claims the kids they want to control in battle.

The host walks the party and runs cutscenes. Friends see the host's screen live and pick moves on their kids' turns.
